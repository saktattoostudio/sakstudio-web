Song

Owner & Lead Artist — US-trained

Friendly, a little bit weird (the good kind), and running low on energy by evening — feed him coffee or a cold beer and he's all yours. 10+ years of experience across the US and Bangkok.

My journey took me from the bustling streets of Los Angeles, where I worked at Generation8 Tattoo — one of the city's busiest shops. There, I immersed myself in realism, fine-line, black-and-grey, and color work. It was during this time that I discovered my true passion: single-needle work, captivated by the precision of highly realistic black-and-grey tattoos.

When the pandemic slowed LA's scene, I moved to New York City — working as a guest artist in Brooklyn, then full-time at Ten Thousand Foxes in Queens, where Tim and Mama helped me refine my client communication. A special thank you to Yuihwa at Blindreason Tattoo in Manhattan for welcoming me onto their team. New York was a period of immense growth, in skill and artistry.

After New York, I followed my dreams to Miami — a place that first inspired me — freelancing and guesting across the city, then traveling the US: Boston, Washington DC, Chicago, Austin, San Francisco, Portland, Seattle, Utah, and Arizona. Every stop was exhilarating and enriching.

Now I'm back in my hometown of Bangkok, ready to bring everything I've learned back to where it all began. In April 2024, I opened Sak Studio — a place that embodies my passion and my commitment to exceptional art. My goal is to give you a comfortable, confident experience and tattoos you'll cherish forever.

Feel free to reach out and let's create something amazing together!

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Book online at book.sakstudio.art — pick your size, style, date and time, and tell us your idea. A small 1,500 ฿ deposit holds your slot and counts toward your final price. We confirm by email within 12 hours. Prefer to chat first? Message us on Instagram @sakstudio.bkk.
From 1,500 ฿. Final price depends on design, size and placement — we always give you a range up front, and the highest charge won't exceed it.
Eat a proper meal 3+ hours before, sleep well, and shower beforehand. Skip alcohol and sun exposure, keep caffeine to one cup, and moisturise your skin in the days leading up.
If you're pregnant, on antibiotics or steroids, vaccinated within 48 hours, under 18, intoxicated, or without government ID. We'll always ask first.
